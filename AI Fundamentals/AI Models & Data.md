**_TRAINING DATA_**

To train ML models on data, we need to learn more about the source of the data, to ensure that the data itself is upto quality.




**_Sources of data_**

**web scraping:** collecting data from websites acorss the internet. _trust profile: *low* no curator, no version control, content will change after collection_

**licensed datasets:** data purchased or agreed with platforms. _trust profile: *medium* terms are often unclear, original users might not have consented to AI training use._

**Synthetic data:** AI generated content that is used to further train the data. _trust profile *variable*  growing fast_

**Internal corpora:** company knowledge bases, support transcripts, clinical notes used for fine-tuning _trust profile *higher* organisation has direct control but is completely liable in case of a mishap._




**_The problem of provenance_**

it is the ability to answer 3 questions about any piece of training data.

_1. where did the data come from?_
_2. when was the data collected?_
_3. has it been modified since?_
